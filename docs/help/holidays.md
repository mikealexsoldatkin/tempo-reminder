# Holidays

A holiday is not a working day: nobody has to log time for it, and the scheduled check does not run
on it either. The two switches at the top decide what counts as a day off — skipping weekends, and
taking the holiday calendar into account — and both save the moment you flip them. With the
calendar switched off the list below changes nothing, and holidays become ordinary working days.

Holidays are stored as **rules**, not dates, so the calendar does not go stale: "last Monday of May"
is still the right Monday next year. **Add holiday** asks for a name and how it repeats — a fixed
day of a month, or the n-th weekday of a month. **Restore defaults** brings back the shipped
calendar, and **Remove selected** clears the ones you tick.

The **shift in days** field is for days that hang off another holiday. The day after Thanksgiving is
"4th Thursday of November, plus one day" — not the 4th Friday, which in some years is a different
date altogether.
